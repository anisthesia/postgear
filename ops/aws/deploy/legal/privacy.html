Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

social.ridepro.in is a private, self-hosted social media scheduling tool. It is operated by RidePro for its own team and the organisations it manages. It is not offered to the general public.

What we collect

  • Account details: the email address, name and hashed password of each team member.
  • Connected social accounts: when you connect YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn or X, we store the access tokens those platforms issue, plus the account name, ID and profile picture they return.
  • Content you create: post text, images and videos you upload, schedules, and comments left on post previews.
  • Platform metrics: analytics the connected platforms return for your own pages and channels.

How we use it

We use this data only to schedule and publish posts on the accounts you connect, and to show you their analytics. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except the social platforms you choose to publish to.

Google user data

When you connect a YouTube channel, we request access to upload videos, manage your channel's content and read its analytics. We use that access only to publish the content you schedule and to display your channel's statistics. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

All data is stored on our own server in the AWS Mumbai region (ap-south-1). Access is restricted to the operator.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting a social account in the app deletes its stored tokens. To have your account and content deleted, or to revoke our access, email us at the address below. You can also revoke Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
